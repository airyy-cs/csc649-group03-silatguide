# SilatGuide – Sources and Verification Status

This file lists every source cited in `silat_qa.csv`, **what was actually checked**, and what the
group still has to verify by hand. Please read the status column honestly before the
presentation: *the AI assistant that drafted this dataset could read web pages, but it is not a
substitute for the group checking each source themselves.*

Checking was done on **21 November 2026**. Pages can change, so open each link again and record
your own date in the "Group re-check" column.

**Status legend**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Page read** | The full text of the page was opened and read during drafting. |
| **Snippet only** | Only a search-result excerpt/abstract was seen. The page itself still needs to be opened and read. |
| **Team guidance** | Not a quotation from a source: practical advice written by the drafter that *combines* the listed sources. Needs a human decision. |

## Entries that need manual academic verification

| Dataset question | Why it needs a human check |
|---|---|
| Is Silat practised only in Malaysia? | S8/S9 were seen only as excerpts; confirm wording and that Brunei/Thailand/Singapore claims are accurate. |
| How far back can Silat be traced…? | JWN text also says "until the Melaka Sultanate (13th century)". Historians date Melaka's founding to around 1400, so the *century* in that JWN sentence looks inconsistent. The dataset therefore avoids giving a Melaka century. Ask the lecturer/a historian how to treat this. |
| What are the different types of Silat…? | UNESCO says "more than 150 known styles"; a Star/Bernama report (S6) says "over 1,000 types". The answer cites UNESCO and mentions that other reports differ (note A). |
| What are some well-known Silat styles or schools…? | Names come from an OCR'd scanned letter (S7) and a news excerpt (S6). Check spellings and whether each is a *style* or an *association*. |
| Which organisations help protect Silat…? | Role wording ("coordinated the country's heritage work") is a summary of S5 and the periodic-report snippet; confirm. |
| How was Silat nominated…? | Check that the nomination was submitted on 30 March **2018** (S5 says "March 30 last year" in a 2019 article). |
| What is Bunga Silat? / langkah / jurus | Sourced to one style organisation (S15). Confirm with an academic source or your instructor; other schools use different counts and terms. |
| Why is discipline important…? | Combines S1 and S14; the logical link ("all depend on following the instructor's guidance") is the drafter's inference. |
| Which parts of the body… / Is sparring riskier… / Why is warming up… | Numbers are from one regional study of adolescent *competition* athletes in Indonesia (S14). Verify against the full paper and say so in your talk. |
| How can beginners learn Silat safely? / Is sparring riskier (last sentence) / Can I learn Silat for health and fitness? / What is a keris… (last sentence) | **Team guidance**, not quotations. They are sensible general safety advice, but a qualified instructor or sports-medicine source should review them. |
| What is a keris and do beginners use it? | "Traditional Malay dagger" is general knowledge not tied to a specific fetched source; add a citation. |
| What do Silat practitioners traditionally wear? | Dress varies by school; the entry says so. Check against your own school's practice. |

## Notes

* **A – style count.** The dataset prefers the UNESCO figure and says that news reports differ.
* **Wikipedia** appeared in search results and was deliberately *not* used as a dataset source.
* **Indonesian vs Malaysian entries.** UNESCO inscribed two separate elements in 2019 (Malaysia: *Silat*, 01504; Indonesia: *Traditions of Pencak Silat*, 01391). Ethics answers draw on the Indonesian entry for value statements and say "related Pencak Silat tradition". Don't present those as Malaysia-specific without checking.
* No personal data, names of living individuals or contact details are in the dataset.
